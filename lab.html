Four kinds of processing.

Computers don’t just store stuff — they process it. These four moves show up in every game, app, and program. Flip, compare, search, and count. Same ideas, made playable.

01 · If/Then

If something is true, do something else.

A computer checks a condition. If it’s yes, it runs one path. If it’s no, it runs the other. Flip the switch and watch the robot choose.
