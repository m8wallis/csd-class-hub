A zombie apocalypse… but make it pastry

Bake Before Dark

Scavenge by day. Bake like your life depends on it. Feed the hungry undead by night.

Who's playing?

Game length

Endless phases. Each phase is a 90 second day and a 90 second night. How long can you last?

How to play

  1. ☀️ Day: explore town. Grab ingredients, weapons, and 🩹 first aid.
  2. 🔥 Oven: bring ingredients to the bakery during the day (it's off at night). Mix 2–3 to discover recipes. You start knowing 🍪 Sugar Cookie, and 📜 recipe scrolls hidden around town teach you more.
  3. 🌙 Night: zombies swarm. Throw treats for a food coma… but some treats make them ANGRY.
  4. 🏏 Weapons stun zombies, and enough hits knock them out for good. Some treats hit hard too. From phase 6, hunt for the 🔪 Machete.
  5. 💖 The cure: a legendary treat made from rare ingredients turns a zombie back into a person.
  6. ♾️ Endless: every phase brings more zombies, faster and tougher. After phase 5, watch for 🪤 bear traps and deadly 🕳️ pits!

Points: +1 per stun · +3 per knockout · +30 per cure · +5 per new recipe (+25 for finding them all) · +5, 10, 15… for surviving each phase

Controls

  • WASD / Arrows move
  • Space swing weapon
  • F or click throw treat
  • Q / 1–9 pick treat
  • E use oven / swap weapon
  • B recipe book
  • P pause · M mute
